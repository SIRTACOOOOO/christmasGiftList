🎄 Christmas Gift List

Everyone's Christmas wishlists, all in one place.

Christmas Gift List is a free, easy-to-use wishlist and gift coordination platform designed to make Christmas shopping easier for families and friends.

Create wishlists, share them with groups, chat about gift ideas, organize Secret Santa exchanges, and keep track of your Christmas shopping!

🌐 Website: "sirtacooooo.github.io/christmasGiftList" (https://sirtacooooo.github.io/christmasGiftList/)

---

✨ Features

🎁 Personal Wishlists

- Create and manage your own Christmas wishlist.
- Add gifts with names, prices, website links, and notes. Enter the link first and the site offers to fill in the name and price.
- Share a product page from your phone's browser straight into the app (installed app, see SETUP.md).
- Set the priority of your gifts by arranging them in your preferred order.
- Sort gifts by priority, date added, website, name, or price.
- Mark gifts as bought.
- Optionally move purchased gifts to the bottom of the list.
- View product previews when available.

👥 Groups

- Create groups with family and friends.
- Invite people using group invitation codes or QR codes.
- Join multiple groups.
- Browse everyone's wishlists in your groups.
- Each group is its own dropdown, with its people listed by full name and profile.
- Manage group membership.
- Keep everyone organized in one place.

💬 Group Chat

- Chat with members of your groups directly.
- Send anonymous messages.
- Mention other members using "@mentions".
- Reply to a specific message, and see unread indicators (a New messages line, counts on each group, and a badge on the app icon).
- Phone push notifications for new messages, @mentions, replies and Secret Santa draws (optional, see SETUP.md).
- Stretch the chat sideways on a computer; on a phone it shrinks above the keyboard.
- Messages automatically disappear after 30 days.

🎅 Secret Santa

- Organize Secret Santa exchanges within your groups.
- Automatically draw recipients.
- Prevent duplicate assignments and drawing yourself.
- Allow participants to draw multiple names.
- Set an optional spending limit.
- Keep assignments private, including from the organizer.

💰 Budget Tracking

- Set a personal overall budget.
- Track money reserved for gifts.
- Track money already spent.
- See how much of your budget remains.
- Keep your personal budget private.
- Track spending against Secret Santa limits.

👤 Accounts & Profiles

- Create an account and securely log in.
- Add and switch between multiple accounts on the same device.
- Customize your profile picture.
- Set your birthday.
- Change your username and password.
- Recover your account if you forget your password.

🎨 Themes & Customization

- Press the theme icon to cycle through the themes (Green is the default).
- Press the sun / moon icon to switch between light and dark mode.
- Swipe right for the menu, swipe left for the chat.
- On phones the themes that draw a box around the list drop it, so the list gets the whole screen.
- Customize your countdown.
- Choose between Christmas, birthday, or no countdown.
- Install the website as an app on supported devices.

📦 Import, Export & Printing (in Settings)

- Export your wishlist to CSV.
- Import gifts from CSV files.
- Create JSON backups of your wishlist.
- Restore your wishlist from a JSON backup.
- Print your wishlist or save it as a PDF.

🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit "Christmas Gift List" (https://sirtacooooo.github.io/christmasGiftList/).
2. Create an account using an invitation sign-up code.
3. Create your wishlist and start adding gifts.
4. Create a group or join an existing one.
5. Invite family and friends.
6. Browse wishlists, chat, and organize your Christmas shopping!

💡 Why Christmas Gift List?

Christmas shopping can get complicated when everyone has different gift ideas and multiple people are buying presents.

Christmas Gift List brings everything together in one place.

Instead of sharing spreadsheets, sending endless messages, or trying to remember what everyone wants, your family and friends can organize everything through one shared platform.
