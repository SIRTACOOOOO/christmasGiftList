# 🎄 Christmas Gift List

A simple, easy-to-use wishlist platform that makes organizing Christmas gifts with family and friends easier!

**Website:** https://sirtacooooo.github.io/christmasGiftList/

## ✨ Features

### 🎁 Personal Wishlists
- Create and manage your own Christmas wishlist.
- Add gifts with names, prices, website links, and notes.
- Keep track of gifts you want and gifts you've purchased.
- Organize your gift ideas in one convenient place.

### 👥 Groups
- Create groups with family and friends.
- Invite others to join your group.
- Browse everyone's wishlists in one place.
- Make Christmas shopping easier for everyone.

### 🛍️ Gift Management
- Keep track of gifts across multiple people.
- See which gifts have been purchased.
- Organize your shopping without losing track of ideas.

### 🎨 Customization
- Customize the appearance of your experience.
- Enjoy a Christmas-themed interface.
- Access your wishlists from any device with a web browser.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit [Christmas Gift List](https://sirtacooooo.github.io/christmasGiftList/).
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Create your wishlist.
4. Add gifts you'd like to receive.
5. Create or join a group with family and friends.
6. Start planning your Christmas shopping!

## 💡 Why Christmas Gift List?

Christmas shopping can get complicated when everyone has different gift ideas and multiple people are buying presents.

Christmas Gift List brings everything together in one place, helping families and friend groups organize wishlists and make gift-giving easier.
